Description

Soft, sweet, and just a little addictive—Sunday Muse is made for those effortlessly stylish moments with your favorite people. Think coffee dates, spontaneous brunches, or golden-hour walks through the city. This scent is playful yet elegant, cozy yet confident. It opens with a juicy burst of pear, a pop of pink pepper, and delicate orange blossom. The heart surprises with a bold blend of coffee, jasmine, bitter almond, and licorice—a deliciously warm and intriguing twist. As it settles, creamy vanilla, earthy patchouli, and smooth cashmere wood and cedar wrap around you like your favorite oversized sweater. A scent for the ones who laugh loud, live fully, and leave behind a little magic wherever they go.

Directions for use

Spray the perfume on your pulse points such as sides of the neck and wrists for all day freshness.
